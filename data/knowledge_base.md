# Knowledge Base

## Password and account access

- Users can reset their password from the login screen by selecting "Forgot password".
- The password reset link expires after 30 minutes.
- If the user no longer has access to their registered email, the case must be escalated to the account verification team.

## Payments

- Payments can be made using credit or debit cards.
- A declined payment should be retried only after verifying the card information.
- Support agents cannot manually approve declined payments.

## Refunds

- Refunds may be requested within 10 calendar days of purchase.
- A refund is not available if more than 30% of the course has been completed.
- Approved refunds may take between 5 and 7 business days.

## Certificates

- Certificates become available after completing 100% of the course.
- The user must pass all mandatory evaluations.
